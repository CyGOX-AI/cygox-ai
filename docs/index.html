About CYGOX AI

CYGOX AI is an algorithmic trading company. It builds AI-powered trading bots that run around the clock inside a client's own broker account, and it operates four of them in production: Velocity, Bullion, Keystone and Atlas. Between them they cover the major currency pairs, gold, and US equities and index ETFs.

The company's distinguishing principle is that nothing on its website should have to be taken on trust. Each bot runs a public, real-money live account — not a demo — whose trades stream in real time to independent third-party auditors. The whole record, losses included, is open to anyone without an account or a login. This page collects those records, explains how the system works, and shows how to check every figure yourself. The primary source is always the official CYGOX AI website.

How it works

  1. Your account, your broker. You open and fund a broker account in your own name. CYGOX AI never takes custody of client money.
  2. A trade-only connection. A bot is connected with trade-only access: it can open and close positions, and that is the entire extent of what the connection permits. Withdrawals and transfers through it are technically impossible.
  3. Systematic execution. Every entry, exit and position size follows the bot's rules — no discretion, no emotion, no exceptions.
  4. Two records of every fill. Each trade appears in your broker dashboard and in the public FXBlue record, both in real time, so they can always be cross-checked.
  5. You stay in control. You can switch the bots off or disconnect them through your broker at any moment, without asking anyone.

Verify it yourself — a checklist

If you are evaluating CYGOX AI, check the claims directly rather than relying on any summary, this page included.

  • Open the FXBlue records above. They are published by FXBlue, not by the company. Losing trades and drawdowns are visible next to the wins.
  • Look at drawdown before return. Velocity's peak drawdown is −10.6% and Atlas's −8.9%. Losing trades and drawdown periods appear in every record.
  • Compare with your own broker statement. Because the bots trade inside your account, your broker holds an independent second copy of the same fills.
  • Check custody. CYGOX AI never holds client funds. Anyone asking you to send money to them directly is not CYGOX AI.
  • Check the domain. The only official website is cygox.com; the only official support address is support@cygox.com.

Frequently asked questions

What is CYGOX AI?

CYGOX AI is an algorithmic trading company that builds AI-powered trading bots which execute 24/7 inside a client's own broker account. It operates four production bots: Velocity, Bullion, Keystone and Atlas.

Who verifies CYGOX AI's trading results?

FXBlue and Myfxbook, two independent third-party trade-verification platforms that CYGOX AI does not control. Every bot runs a public, real-money live account whose trades stream to them in real time, straight from the broker.

Does CYGOX AI hold client funds?

No. Funds stay in the client's own broker account, in the client's name. The connection is trade-only: the bots can open and close positions, and withdrawals or transfers through it are technically impossible.

Which markets do the CYGOX AI bots trade?

Velocity trades EUR/USD and GBP/USD. Bullion trades gold (XAU/USD). Keystone trades large-cap US stocks and major index ETFs during regular US market hours. Atlas trades a diversified basket of currency pairs.

Yes. Every published record includes losing trades and drawdown periods, and algorithmic trading carries a substantial risk of loss. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Where can I see the CYGOX AI live track records?

On FXBlue, at the four public account pages for Velocity, Bullion, Keystone and Atlas, and consolidated on the performance page at cygox.com/performance. No account or login is needed to read them.
