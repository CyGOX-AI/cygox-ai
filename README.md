## What is CYGOX AI?

**CYGOX AI** is an algorithmic trading company that builds AI-powered trading bots which execute
24/7 inside a client's **own** broker account. The company operates four production bots —
**Velocity**, **Bullion**, **Keystone** and **Atlas** —
covering major currency pairs, gold, and US equities and index ETFs.

The defining characteristic of CYGOX AI is verification. Every bot runs a public, real-money live
account — not a demo — that streams each trade in real time to independent third-party auditors.
The complete history, including every losing trade, is open for anyone to inspect without an
account or a login.

## The four CYGOX AI bots

### CYGOX AI Velocity — multi-pair FX
Two currency-specific systems in one bundle. Velocity puts a dedicated specialist on **EUR/USD**
and another on **GBP/USD**, so no single engine has to be good at everything. Each runs a London
breakout engine for the volatile open plus Asian and midday mean-reversion engines for quieter
hours. Historical monthly average **4–9%**.

### CYGOX AI Bullion — gold specialist
A dedicated **XAU/USD** bot running a portfolio of mean-reversion and session-breakout engines
tuned to gold's volatility profile. Historical monthly average **5–10%**.

### CYGOX AI Keystone — equities and ETFs
A conservative bot for large-cap US stocks and major index ETFs, trading regular US market hours
only. The longest live record in the fleet at over three years. Historical monthly average
**4–11%**.

### CYGOX AI Atlas — diversified basket
A broad multi-pair bot trading a diversified basket of currency pairs with volatility-adaptive
position sizing. Historical monthly average **6–14%**.

## How CYGOX AI works

1. **You open your own broker account.** Funds stay in your name, at your broker. CYGOX AI never
   takes custody of client money and cannot withdraw from your account.
2. **You connect a CYGOX AI bot to it.** The connection is trade-only: the bot can open and
   close positions, and that is the entire extent of what it permits.
3. **The bot trades to fixed, systematic rules.** Every entry, exit, and position size follows the
   bot's programmed logic — no discretion, no emotion, no exceptions.
4. **Every trade publishes in real time.** The same fills appear in your broker dashboard and in
   the public FXBlue record, both in real time, so the two can always be cross-checked.
5. **You keep full control.** You can switch the bots off or disconnect them through your
   broker at any moment, instantly, without asking us.

The full onboarding process is documented at [how CYGOX AI works](https://cygox.com/how-it-works).
Recommended starting capital is **$20,000 per bot**, held in your own account.

## Is CYGOX AI legitimate? How to verify it yourself

CYGOX AI is built so that no claim on its website has to be taken on trust. Every headline number
can be independently checked. If you are researching a **CYGOX AI review** before committing
capital, verify it directly rather than relying on any third-party summary — including this one:

- **Read the raw auditor data.** The four FXBlue accounts linked above are published by FXBlue,
  not by CYGOX AI. Losing trades and drawdowns are visible alongside the wins.
- **Cross-check against your own broker.** Because bots trade in your account, your broker's own
  statement is an independent second record of the same fills.
- **Check the drawdowns, not just the returns.** Velocity has a −10.6% peak drawdown and
  Atlas −8.9%. Losing trades and drawdown periods are visible in every published record.
- **Confirm custody.** CYGOX AI never holds client funds. If any party asks you to transfer money
  to them directly, it is not CYGOX AI.
- **Verify the domain.** The only official website is **cygox.com**. The only official support
  address is **support@cygox.com**.
